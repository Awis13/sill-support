Sill

A glass thermometer for Bluetooth temperature and humidity sensors.

Support

Questions, bug reports or a sensor that does not show up: open an issue at github.com/Awis13/sill-support/issues. Please include the sensor model and the iOS version.

Works with

  • Xiaomi Mijia LYWSDCGQ (the round one with the e-ink screen)
  • Xiaomi, Qingping and similar sensors running ATC1441 or pvvx custom firmware
  • BTHome v2 sensors
  • Several Govee models (they update while the app is open)

Sill is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Xiaomi, Govee, Qingping or the BTHome project; their names are used only to say which sensors work.
