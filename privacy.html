Sill privacy policy

Effective 16 October 2026

The short version

Sill does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What the app does with data

  • Bluetooth. Sill listens to the temperature, humidity and battery values that nearby thermometers broadcast. It never connects to or pairs with them. These readings, the names you give your thermometers and your settings are kept only on your device (and shared between Sill, its widgets and its Apple Watch and Mac apps through an app group on your own devices).
  • Apple Intelligence. The short line about the air is written by Apple's on-device language model. The reading is processed on your device and does not leave it.
  • Tips. Sill is free. If you leave an optional tip, the payment is handled entirely by Apple through in-app purchase. Sill never sees your payment details or identity.

Data retention and deletion

Everything Sill keeps is on your device. Forget a thermometer in Settings to remove it, or delete the app to remove all of it. Because nothing is sent to us, there is nothing for us to delete on a server.

Third parties

Sill contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code, and shares nothing with third parties.

Children

Sill collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. Questions: open an issue at github.com/Awis13/sill-support/issues.
